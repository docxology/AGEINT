#### Lesson 6: Active Inference and Predictive Processing as Models for Cognitive Attack/Defense

**Concept.** **Active Inference and Predictive Processing as Models for Cognitive Attack/Defense** treats the free-energy principle as computational-neuroscience theory. Define the generative model, variational approximation, and observations; distinguish prediction error from variational free energy and posterior approximation from evidence that the model is true. An internally consistent update does not establish deception or factual reliability.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** Active inference supplies a formal modeling vocabulary for beliefs, observations and action under explicit assumptions; a model fit does not uniquely identify a human mechanism or hostile influence. The undergraduate review and broader theoretical material do not validate a cognitive-attack detector, an individual cognitive score or a human/agent isomorphism. Compare model predictions with alternatives rather than diagnose real people [@ageint106; @ageint003].

**Why it matters.** Analysts use **Active Inference** to separate analysis of influence from persuasion design.
