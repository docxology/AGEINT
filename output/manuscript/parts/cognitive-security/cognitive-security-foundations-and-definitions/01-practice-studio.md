#### Lesson 1: Cognitive security definitions and scope

**Concept.** **Cognitive security definitions and scope** maps cognitive attack surfaces: attention, belief formation, narrative provenance, and transparent correction options.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** Cognitive security concerns conditions that protect informed, autonomous thinking and decision-making against information-related harms; definitions differ over whether the unit is a person, organization, or information environment. Cognitive warfare is a contested strategic/military framing, not a synonym for every cognitive-security problem: an ordinary mistaken notice does not establish an adversarial campaign. Neither term licenses deciding which opinions people may hold or treating biological/psychological/social labels as a universally validated taxonomy. The research article and its LinkedIn announcement are one underlying publication, not independent corroboration [@ageint165; @ageint167]; compare definitional scope rather than counting URLs.

A defensible treatment names the judgment it enables for information-integrity analysis and resilience education review, the proof limit that treating resilience labels as permission to skip provenance review would otherwise hide, and the reviewer accountable for challenge.

**Concept.** **Defining Comprehensive Cognitive Security in the Digital Era (SSRN 2024)** maps cognitive attack surfaces: attention, belief formation, narrative provenance, and transparent correction options.
