#### Lesson 1: Understanding Neurocognitive Mechanisms of Cognitive Security (Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews, 2025)

**Concept.** **Understanding Neurocognitive Mechanisms of Cognitive Security (Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews, 2025)** maps cognitive attack surfaces: attention, belief formation, narrative provenance, and transparent correction options.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** The review proposes a framework spanning executive, affective, and integrative mechanisms and explicitly says the drivers and neurophysiology are incompletely understood. A mechanism hypothesis explains a possible process; a candidate measure is an observation proposed for study; a validated biomarker requires evidence for a specified use. Publisher and PubMed records refer to the same review, not independent replications, and the framework does not validate person-level vulnerability, credibility, or diagnostic judgments [@ageint176; @ageint177].
