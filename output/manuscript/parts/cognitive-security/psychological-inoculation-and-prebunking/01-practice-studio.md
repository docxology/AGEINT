#### Lesson 1: Psychological inoculation and prebunking literacy

**Concept.** **Psychological inoculation and prebunking literacy** uses inoculation methods as evidence-informed, bounded, and context-dependent resilience education with transparent labels, source checks, non-manipulative corrections, and explicit measurement limits.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** Psychological inoculation uses a forewarning and a refutation of a weakened misleading argument or technique before later exposure; the medical analogy does not establish immunity, a clinical effect, or protection against every future claim. Distinguish technique recognition, factual discernment, sharing intention, observed behavior, and durability. The cited contemporary reviews discuss the theory's history; they are not themselves the 1961 primary experiments [@ageint181; @ageint182].

**Why it matters.** **Psychological inoculation** matters in the **Cognitive Security and Influence Resilience** lane because information-integrity analysis and resilience education evidence must stay separate from judgment; treating resilience labels as permission to skip provenance review is a common failure.
