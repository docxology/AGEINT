#### Lesson 1: The Cognitive Attack Surface: Attention, Memory, Trust, and Decision-Making

**Concept.** **The Cognitive Attack Surface: Attention, Memory, Trust, and Decision-Making** maps cognitive attack surfaces: attention, belief formation, narrative provenance, and transparent correction options.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** Attack surface is a defensive organizing metaphor here: competing demands may affect attention, repetition may affect familiarity, authority cues may affect trust, and incomplete information may affect decisions. These are hypotheses about processes, not observations of anyone's private mental state. Ordinary error, satire, accessibility problems, and legitimate disagreement remain alternatives; the policy paper does not validate a detector for manipulation or person-level susceptibility [@ageint095].
