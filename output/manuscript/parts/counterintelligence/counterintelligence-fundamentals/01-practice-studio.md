#### Lesson 6: Polygraph: Source-vetting and insider-threat review using sample personnel records

**Concept.** **Polygraph: Source-vetting and insider-threat review using sample personnel records** connects insider-threat review to access control, behavior indicators, and accountable escalation paths.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** Physiological responses are not uniquely deception; distinguish specific-event testing from broad screening and do not infer guilt from a positive screen. The National Research Council's 2003 executive summary cautions against generalizing specific-incident research to screening and documents serious screening limits (https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/10420/chapter/2); ICD 203 is not polygraph-validity evidence. For a separate hypothetical arithmetic exercise, assume 1,000 cases, 10 true target events, sensitivity 80% and specificity 90%: TP=8, FN=2, FP=99, TN=891, so only 8/107 positive screens are true positives. These assumed rates are not measured polygraph performance; the defensible answer is no adverse-person conclusion and no learner testing or evasion instruction.
