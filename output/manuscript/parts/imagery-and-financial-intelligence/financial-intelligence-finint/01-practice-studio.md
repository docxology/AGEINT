#### Lesson 1: FININT Foundations: Collection, Analysis, Reporting

**Concept.** **FININT Foundations: Collection, Analysis, Reporting** reads financial intelligence as due-diligence evidence that triggers review rather than proof of intent or guilt.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** FININT analysis separates transaction observations, entity-resolution hypotheses and claims about purpose or legality. A payment, a network edge and a beneficial-owner conclusion require different evidence; access to a public registry does not authorize unrestricted aggregation or prove the registry is complete or current. The two inherited encyclopedia links share a source family and are not two independent authorities for a financial or legal conclusion.

**Why it matters.** **FININT Foundations** connects classroom vocabulary to Financial Intelligence and Economic-Security Due Diligence practice: learners document evidence, caveats, and reviewer ownership rather than repeating labels.

#### Lesson 3: Non-state actor indicator review with attribution caution and governance boundaries

**Concept.** **Non-state actor indicator review with attribution caution and governance boundaries** uses attribution indicators cautiously by separating technical similarity, context, confidence, and geopolitical inference.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** A typology describes a possible pattern, not terrorist purpose, a prohibited recipient or criminal conduct; subscriptions and donations can explain repeated small payments. In a fictional packet, three 20-unit transfers on consecutive dates have no list match, while invoice I1 explains two as scheduled instalments and the third lacks context. The result is 'two transfers explained by supplied invoices; third unresolved', not 'clean because no hit' or 'terrorist financing because repeated'; currency, time and counterparty context are still required. A list non-match cannot bypass transaction-context review, and a red flag cannot establish guilt. No interdiction, live monitoring or operational response follows from the classroom disposition.
