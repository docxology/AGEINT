#### Active Inference and AGEINT knowledge architecture: inputs, transforms, outputs, and failure checks

- **Inputs:** synthetic states, prior probabilities, observation likelihoods, model version, and explicit assumptions. [@ageint247]; [@ageint248].
- **Transforms:** normalize prior-times-likelihood weights, compare alternative models, inspect sensitivity, and distinguish inference from policy evaluation.
- **Outputs:** model-conditional posterior table, assumption ledger, model-adequacy gap, and human-reviewed theory-to-evidence card.
- **Failure modes:** treating a consistent posterior as truth, equating free energy with prediction error, double-counting copied evidence, or inferring hostile intent from model surprise.
