#### Lesson 1: Financial due-diligence typology exercise using synthetic records and compliance boundaries

**Concept.** **Financial due-diligence typology exercise using synthetic records and compliance boundaries** structures financial-pattern review as typology-driven due diligence with escalation thresholds, not proof of wrongdoing.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** These are overlapping descriptors with different evidentiary and legal requirements, not mutually exclusive bins or a scale of guilt. Commercial activity or advocacy is not inherently hostile; 'proxy' requires a specified relationship, distinguishing support, alignment, tasking, direction and control. Code observed conduct separately from organizational self-description and contested labels [@ageint012].

**Why it matters.** Analysts use **Financial due-diligence typology exercise** to separate collection discipline concepts from operational recruitment, interception, or tasking procedures. A defensible treatment names the judgment it enables for requirements decomposition and source-discipline fit review, the proof limit that treating typology match as proof of intent would otherwise hide, and the reviewer accountable for challenge.
