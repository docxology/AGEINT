### human-source literacy and source protection practice artifact: recurring packet and retained evidence

**Navigation.** Parent section: [@sec:part-human-intelligence-humint].

The recurring practice artifact is a **source-risk and validation ethics worksheet** that draws on validation note, pressure-risk checklist, source-protection boundary, and oversight escalation memo. The unit keeps its learning spine explicit. Classroom packet: fictional interview summaries H1 and H2 both report a training-room closure; H1 saw a sign, H2 repeats H1, and a dated facilities card says closure starts next week. A motivation card describes H1 as eager to help.

1. Extract each reported observation, access claim, and date without retaining personal identifiers.
2. Mark H2 as dependent reporting; treat helpfulness as a motivation hypothesis, not proof of accuracy, consent, or authority.
3. Compare the summaries with the facilities card and test the alternative that the interview omitted the closure date.
4. Complete the validation worksheet: current closure is unsupported, next week's scheduled closure has documentary support, and what H1 actually saw remains unresolved. Removing H2 must not reduce the independent-source count.
Do not contact or pressure a source to resolve the gap. Stop if a summary exposes sensitive details; hand a minimized discrepancy note to the instructor's source-protection reviewer.

### human-source literacy and source protection safety boundary: accountable, synthetic, and evidence-bounded limits

No elicitation, impersonation, covert contact, or operational source-management procedures; all cases use classroom fixtures.

This unit introduces the part's governing question, evidence artifacts, source-support spine, and capstone thread before the individual modules begin. [@ageint238]; [@ageint239].

Learners carry one unit capstone thread through the part: define an accountable intelligence question, bind it to source-quality constraints, produce a reviewable artifact, test the artifact against failure modes, and hand it off with enough context for another analyst or instructor to audit. The capstone remains public, synthetic, or owned-lab throughout; its first source anchors are [@ageint238]; [@ageint239].

This unit's deliverables are a source-canon card, claim/evidence ledger, safe-practice lab packet, failure-mode note, instructor rubric, and debrief memo. The full source-lane and evidence-package ledgers appear in the orientation and appendices; this unit introduction keeps only the learner-facing spine for [@ageint238]; [@ageint239].

This unit's safety gates are documented authority for the exact activity, rights review, permitted data provenance, tool allowlisting, human oversight, and evidence-bounded output. A missing or unknown gate pauses the affected activity, including processing or sharing already supplied data. A written risk exception cannot waive authority or rights. A replacement tabletop may begin only after independently permitted synthetic inputs, tools and review responsibilities are established; it does not inherit permission from the stopped activity. Inspect the applicable source context: [@ageint238]; [@ageint239].

Capstone thread:

| Phase | Artifact | Review gate |
|---|---|---|
| Question | accountable learning question and excluded-action list | instructor confirms scope, allowed data, and rights impact |
| Source canon | source-lane map with guide citations, verified anchors, and refresh dates | citation keys resolve and source identity lock remains stable |
| Evidence ledger | claim, evidence, uncertainty, confidence, and reviewer register | every material claim has a source, caveat, and owner |
| Safe lab | synthetic or public dataset packet, tool allowlist, and stop conditions | no live target, private data, external action, or unsafe cyber-physical step |
| Assurance | evaluation rubric, failure-mode drill, rights map, and remediation note | human reviewer signs off before presentation or reuse |
| Debrief | capstone memo with lessons, residual risk, refresh triggers, and handoff owner | all unresolved questions and future approvals are explicit |
