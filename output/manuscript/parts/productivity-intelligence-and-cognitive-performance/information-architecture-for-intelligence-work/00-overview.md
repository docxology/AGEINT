### Information Architecture for Intelligence Work conceptual primer: source context, core model, and reader task

This chapter teaches intelligence productivity as governed cognitive performance: external memory, workload limits, focus conditions, and explicit review handoffs support reviewable judgment without guaranteeing performance under sustained tempo. The chapter uses **Operator-Workload Hygiene Lens** to connect definitions, evidence tests, practice artifacts, and review gates for **Personal Knowledge Management Systems for Intelligence Professionals; Zettelkasten: Linked Atomic Notes and Evidence Boundaries**.

The central distinction is to separate cognitive hygiene from heroics, and workload signals from unreviewed urgency. Core topics include **Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) Systems for Intelligence Professionals; Zettelkasten: Linked Atomic Notes and Evidence Boundaries; Obsidian, Roam Research, LogSeq: Tool-Evaluation Questions**. Each topic covers meaning, evidentiary support, common misconceptions, and safety boundaries.

Governance requirements use verified official, standards, public-domain, or scholarly anchors such as [@official_odni_icd_203]; [@scholarly_heuer_psychology_intelligence_analysis]; [@official_nist_sp_800_161r1]. Technical, theoretical, or empirical statements require direct domain sources and are limited to what those sources establish. [@ageint251]; [@ageint268].

Learners move from vocabulary and the **Operator-Workload Hygiene Lens** distinction through topic lessons on **Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) Systems for Intelligence Professionals** with evidence and misconception checks, then assemble an **operator workload card with queue state, NASA-TLX ratings, focus plan, handoff owner, and rest boundary** with safety and rights gates.

### Information Architecture for Intelligence Work learning outcomes: analytic moves, evidence duties, and transfer

**Navigation.** Parent section: [@sec:chapter-information-architecture-for-intelligence-work].

- Connect **Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) Systems for Intelligence Professionals and Zettelkasten: Linked Atomic Notes and Evidence Boundaries** to **Operator Productivity and Cognitive Performance** by naming shared vocabulary, evidence burden, and audience-facing caveats.
- Build an **operator workload card with queue state, NASA-TLX ratings, focus plan, handoff owner, and rest boundary** that keeps observation, inference, uncertainty, source quality, reviewer decision, and refresh trigger separate.
- Apply the key distinction: separate cognitive hygiene from heroics, and workload signals from unreviewed urgency; show where an apparently useful shortcut would cross that line.
- Diagnose failure modes such as heroic overtime, invisible cognitive debt, context-switch thrash, uncalibrated confidence under load, and skipping handoff review, then write one recovery move for each failure mode that preserves the learning objective.
- Teach the defensive boundary back to a peer: productivity material stays educational and synthetic; it does not prescribe live operational tempo, surveillance of people, or performance coercion.

### Information Architecture for Intelligence Work core vocabulary: source terms, method roles, and safety limits

**Navigation.** Parent section: [@sec:chapter-information-architecture-for-intelligence-work].

| Term | Working definition |
|---|---|
| External memory | a capture system for tasks, sources, and commitments outside working memory; its contents still require accuracy, version, and access checks |
| NASA-TLX | a subjective workload instrument covering mental, physical, and temporal demand, perceived performance, effort, and frustration; not a clinical diagnosis |
| Flow precondition | task clarity, challenge-skill balance, and feedback that may support focused work rather than guarantee it |
| Decision hygiene | habits that preserve evidence review, handoffs, and rest boundaries under load |
| Task switching | a context change that can impose a variable recovery burden; re-entry notes preserve task state without establishing a universal recovery time |
