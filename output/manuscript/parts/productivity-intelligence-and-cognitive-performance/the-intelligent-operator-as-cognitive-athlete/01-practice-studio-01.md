#### Lesson 1: Productivity Tradecraft: Sustainable Work, Review, and Recovery

**Concept.** **Productivity Tradecraft: Sustainable Work, Review, and Recovery** manages workload and bias through prioritization, explicit handoffs, and review checkpoints—not unsustainable pace.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** The cognitive-athlete title is a metaphor, not a brain-fitness assessment or a demand for heroic endurance. Useful output includes correctness, traceability, retained learning, and safe re-entry after interruptions, not simply completed-item counts. Foreign-influence policy sources do not establish productivity mechanisms; this chapter's work-design proposals remain bounded by task fit, accessibility, rest, and non-coercion [@ageint262; @ageint264].

**Why it matters.** Analysts use **Productivity Tradecraft** to separate cognitive hygiene from heroics, and workload signals from unreviewed urgency. A defensible treatment names the judgment it enables for workload-aware operator decision hygiene review, the proof limit that heroic overtime would otherwise hide, and the reviewer accountable for challenge.

#### Lesson 4: The Five GTD Phases: Capture, Clarify, Organize, Reflect, Engage

**Concept.** **The Five GTD Phases: Capture, Clarify, Organize, Reflect, Engage** uses GTD as a decision-hygiene framework: capture, clarify, organize, review, and engage—with explicit authority for what enters analysis.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** **Completed synthetic queue.** At 09:00 capture three supplied items: 'compare archive cards A/B by noon', 'await approved source C', and 'read optional guide'; clarify them as one next action, one waiting-for item, and one someday/reference item, then organize with source IDs and a 10:30 review. At review, engage only in comparing A/B; a 09:15 fictional interruption is parked with the re-entry note 'A/B compared through paragraph 2; check date qualifier next', preserving the scheduled break and independent review. At 10:30 card C arrives with a changed date, so the waiting item becomes 'revise the date comparison' and the draft is held until corrected rather than rushed to meet an item-count target. The handoff contains the next action, last checked source/version, unresolved qualifier, and review time; this completes the workflow without claiming a measured GTD benefit [@ageint198; @ageint197].
