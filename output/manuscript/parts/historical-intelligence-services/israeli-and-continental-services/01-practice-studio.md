Completed offline case: S1 is a synthetic security-service file asserting a person's disloyalty, S2 an independent dated publication contradicting its factual allegation, S3 a later archive release with redactions, and S4 an affected-person correction. Withheld S5 reveals S1 copied an earlier unverified file, so collapse the apparent corroboration and retract the adverse claim; stop person-level inference and retain the correction (see [@sec:analytic-workflow]).
