#### Lesson 1: Executive Order 12333: Version Limits and the Distinct PPD-28 Source

**Concept.** **Executive Order 12333: Version Limits and the Distinct PPD-28 Source** maps the legal source to authority, oversight, retention, and redress fields before any workflow is allowed.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** The inherited citation ageint221 identifies PPD-28, not Executive Order 12333; retain that identity without treating one presidential instrument as the other [@ageint221]. For EO 12333, the existing ODNI text explicitly states amendments in 2003, 2004, and 2008, while the NARA codification displays older DCI-era institutional wording; neither an old page nor an official host alone certifies the current consolidated legal position [@official_eo_12333_odni_text; @official_odni_eo_12333]. The ODNI version's section 2.3 places collection, retention, and dissemination concerning United States persons within specified approved procedures, so its public-information/consent category is not blanket permission for any actor or activity. Application requires current text/status, governed actor, activity, territory/data nexus, time, scope and implementing procedures, reviewed by an authorized legal specialist; this lesson provides no collection authority or legal advice.

**Why it matters.** **Executive Order 12333** connects classroom vocabulary to Legal, Ethical, and Oversight Architecture practice: learners document evidence, caveats, and reviewer ownership rather than repeating labels.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** A statute, executive instrument, judicial authorization, agency directive, voluntary standard, ethical recommendation, and local policy have different sources of force and scope; an analytic-quality directive cannot supply surveillance authority. #### Lesson 3: Intelligence Community Directives: Index Provenance versus Current Text

**Concept.** **Intelligence Community Directives: Index Provenance versus Current Text** compares civilian, military, and technical components by mission, oversight chain, and product type rather than by organizational chart alone.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** An index locates documents; it does not establish that a listed version remains operative, applies to the proposed actor, or authorizes the proposed activity [@ageint006; @ageint298]. Record the issuing authority, document type, revision, effective date, supersession status, applicability, and exact provision before interpretation. ICD 203 addresses analytic standards, so citing it for uncertainty and sourcing is different from citing a lawful basis to obtain or disclose data [@ageint297]. If the required current instrument is missing, the correct disposition is unsupported/hold rather than an inferred permission.

**Why it matters.** Without explicit treatment of **Intelligence Community Directives**, authority laundering through tools undermines authority mapping and rights-aware release review; the lesson builds the habit to separate having a capability from having authority and accountable review.
