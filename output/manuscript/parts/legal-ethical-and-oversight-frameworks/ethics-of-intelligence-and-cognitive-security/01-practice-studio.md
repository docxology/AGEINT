#### Lesson 1: Intelligence Ethics: Deontological, Consequentialist, Virtue Frameworks

**Concept.** **Intelligence Ethics: Deontological, Consequentialist, Virtue Frameworks** focuses on transparent resilience education: provenance, audience harm, attribution caution, and non-manipulative response options.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** Deontological reasoning asks what duties and rights constrain a choice; consequentialist reasoning compares expected benefits and harms with uncertainty; virtue reasoning asks what integrity, restraint, and practical judgment require of the professional. These are normative lenses, not empirical findings supported by the inherited analytic-directive references [@ageint297; @ageint298]. In a fictional release choice, A publishes an unverified anomaly accusation, B circulates a minimized uncertainty-labelled technical note within the approved exercise, and C does no further processing: duties reject unsupported accusation, consequences favor avoiding reputational and availability harms, and integrity requires admitting the evidence gap. The decision owner selects B only because its synthetic data and audience are independently permitted, records dissent favoring C because benefit is unproven, and sets any new audience or real data as a reopening/hold trigger. Lawfulness would be necessary but would not by itself establish ethical acceptability, and expected benefit cannot outweigh a missing authority or non-negotiable rights constraint.

**Why it matters.** Without explicit treatment of **Intelligence Ethics**, treating resilience labels as permission to skip provenance review undermines authority mapping and rights-aware release review; the lesson builds the habit to separate having a capability from having authority and accountable review.

#### Lesson 2: The Ethics of Cognitive Security (YorkSpace Dissertation)

**Concept.** **The Ethics of Cognitive Security (YorkSpace Dissertation)** maps cognitive attack surfaces: attention, belief formation, narrative provenance, and transparent correction options.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** The dissertation is a normative source for examining state intervention in information environments, epistemic paternalism, and the conditions of responsible stewardship, not empirical proof that an intervention works [@ageint172]. Protecting knowledge practices can conflict with autonomy and pluralism; disagreement, error, or distrust alone does not justify treating an audience as a target. In a wholly fictional complaint exercise, a disputed unit-level label is held on receipt of a plain-language, accessible correction request; an independent reviewer acknowledges it by the next teaching session (a classroom target, not a statutory deadline), supplies the relevant reasons and permitted evidence, and finds that two supposed sources copied one report. The reviewer withdraws the label, records correction notices and acknowledgments from approved recipients, offers an accessible further-review route, and closes only after the requester can receive the outcome; absent usable contestability, the decision is hold/redesign, not automatic release. This affected-party remedy differs from an internal safety escalation or protected disclosure.

**Why it matters.** **The Ethics of Cognitive Security (YorkSpace Dissertation)** matters in the **Legal, Ethical, and Oversight Architecture** lane because authority mapping and rights-aware release evidence must stay separate from judgment; treating resilience labels as permission to skip provenance review is a common failure.

#### Lesson 3: AI Ethics in Intelligence: Accountability, Transparency, Proportionality

**Concept.** **AI Ethics in Intelligence: Accountability, Transparency, Proportionality** — Test whether a method is no more intrusive than the accountable need justifies and whether affected groups have redress.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** Necessity asks whether processing is needed for the legitimate aim, suitability whether it can plausibly contribute, and proportionality whether the justified benefit warrants the residual impacts after considering less intrusive alternatives; these are proposed classroom questions, not a universal legal test derived from ICD 203. A completed synthetic options record has aim 'explain a duplicated alert': no-AI manual comparison of two approved cards suffices; an offline summary adds convenience but no demonstrated analytic benefit; external-provider upload adds an unresolved location/retention boundary and is rejected. The decision owner chooses manual comparison, records uncertain benefit and disproportionate disclosure risk for upload, and holds any reuse beyond the approved purpose. The lifecycle record assigns fixture owner F to approved source cards and selected excerpts, analyst A to offline context and derived claim C1, and records reviewer R to the minimized audit row; no provider copy, prompt log, cache, index, or embedding is authorized in this exercise, and discovering one stops processing for review. Fictional policy P1 permits working copies through session closure and only a source-pointer/decision audit row until its next-session review; a later correction to C1 is propagated to the permitted output and audit row before closure. If permission, actual provider processing, retention status, or a preservation duty is unknown, the owner restricts affected reuse and obtains an approved disposition rather than deleting or retaining everything by default; public availability, pseudonymization, or a 'synthetic' label does not cure unknown provenance.

#### Lesson 4: Oversight Mechanisms: Congressional, Inspector General, FISA Court

**Concept.** **Oversight Mechanisms: Congressional, Inspector General, FISA Court** maps the legal source to authority, oversight, retention, and redress fields before any workflow is allowed.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** Project approval, specialist legal/privacy review, independent assurance, inspector-general functions, legislative oversight, and judicial review are different roles with different mandates; none is an interchangeable appeal destination or universal project approver. The inherited ICD index and analytic standard do not establish the current powers, jurisdiction, or procedure of a legislature, inspector general, or FISA court [@ageint298; @ageint297]. In a fictional role record, instructor I controls the exercise boundary, owner O proposes release, reviewer L assesses legal/privacy prerequisites, and independent reviewer R examines disputed evidence; O is then implicated in an unsupported source change, so I pauses affected reuse and sends the minimized decision/source-pointer record to R, not back to O for self-approval. R records 'source authenticity unresolved; release held', asks L to establish the authorized specialist route, and permits re-entry only after evidence and permission are resolved. A named reviewer is insufficient if that reviewer lacks authority, independence, accessibility, or the necessary information.

#### Lesson 5: Whistleblowing, Leaking, and the Epistemic Duty of Intelligence Professionals

**Concept.** **Whistleblowing, Leaking, and the Epistemic Duty of Intelligence Professionals** — Separate lawful disclosure channels, source protection, and oversight from unauthorized release or targeting narratives.

**Worked reasoning and limits.** An epistemic duty to correct error does not create permission for public disclosure, and internal escalation, an affected-party complaint, and a potentially protected disclosure are distinct channels. Applicable protections depend on current jurisdiction, employee/contractor status, authorized recipient/channel, material sensitivity, and governing law; the inherited analytic-directive sources do not establish those protections or promise immunity [@ageint297; @ageint298]. In fictional retention/escalation record D, the analyst identifies an inaccurate derived claim, an approved working-copy deletion trigger, and a possible preservation obligation whose validity and scope are unknown: affected dissemination is stopped, access is restricted to the already authorized reviewers, and only the minimum authorized decision evidence is maintained pending counsel/records disposition. Retention for an approved purpose, preservation against a valid hold, restriction of access/use, and deletion under an approved schedule are separate decisions; a suspected conflict authorizes neither indefinite copying nor destruction. The independent reviewer must resolve the exact hold authority, covered representations, responsible owner, recipients, duration/review trigger, and correction duties before a disposition, using Section [@sec:analytic-workflow]; no real material is acquired or disclosed and no leaking procedure is taught.
