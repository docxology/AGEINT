Source Protection and CI Integration knowledge architecture: inputs, transforms, outputs, and failure checks

  • Inputs: minimized fictional access records, report lineage, source-quality discrepancies, and protected reporting rules. [ageint238]; [ageint241].
  • Transforms: distinguish anomaly from compromise, test benign alternatives, preserve provenance, minimize exposure, and route independent review.
  • Outputs: non-accusatory source-integrity judgment, protected evidence note, correction or hold, and accountable reviewer disposition.
  • Failure modes: suspicion as identity or guilt, relationship trust as accuracy, unauthorized contact, or punitive inference from screening signals.
