Lesson 1: ICS Matrix: Versioned Tactics and Technique Knowledge Base

Concept. ICS Matrix: Versioned Tactics and Technique Knowledge Base is taught in the ICS/OT Cyber-Physical Defense and Tabletop Readiness lane. The analytic distinction is to separate cyber analysis from unsafe process control or live operational action. Apply it to the supplied evidence; a topic label alone establishes neither a mechanism nor a conclusion.

Worked reasoning and limits. ATT&CK for ICS organizes documented adversary behaviors by tactical objective; it is neither an exhaustive catalog of possible behavior nor a mandatory attack sequence [ageint213]. This walkthrough is a selected subset: the inspected v20 matrix (6 November 2026; version permalink https://attack.mitre.org/versions/v20/matrices/ics/) has twelve tactics, including Privilege Escalation, concerning higher-level permissions, and Command and Control, concerning communication with compromised systems, which the inherited subsections do not separately enumerate. Neither an administrative permission nor an ordinary communication alone proves those adversary objectives. Record version and technique identity for each mapping: counts and names can change, and a colored matrix cell is not evidence of local detection or prevention.

Why it matters. Without explicit treatment of ICS Matrix: Versioned Tactics and Technique Knowledge Base, IT-first assumptions undermines safety-aware tabletop readiness and defensive coverage review; the lesson builds the habit to separate cyber analysis from unsafe process control or live operational action.
