Lesson 1: Supply Chain Operations as Strategic Intelligence Tradecraft

Concept. Supply Chain Operations as Strategic Intelligence Tradecraft shows how package provenance, social trust, build integrity, and assurance controls turn a software incident into reviewable evidence.

Worked reasoning and limits. A supply-chain assessment follows trust and artifact dependencies across development, build, distribution and use; it does not teach acquisition operations. Package identity, vulnerability affectedness, deployment and exploitability are separate claims. Analytic standards support distinguishing observations from judgments, not presuming compromise from a supplier relationship [ageint297].
