Worked reasoning and limits. STIX represents observations, indicators, sightings and relationships as distinct assertions; TAXII transports content but neither verifies truth nor authorizes distribution, and TAXII 2.1 specifies Collections while reserving Channels without specifying their services [ageint067; ageint068; ageint310]. In a completed fictional packet, O1 observes artifact H at 09:00, indicator I1 version 1 at 10:00 asserts H is suspicious from source S1, sighting Sg1 at 10:05 references that assertion, O2 copies O1, and relation R1 names an actor without evidence; deduplication leaves one observation lineage, a sourced suspicion and an unsupported actor edge, not three confirmations. I1 version 2 at 11:00 is supplied as revoked, so the historical assertion remains in the record but cannot silently serve as an active indicator; its confidence rationale was a single-source judgment, not an incident probability. A supplied classroom handling card permits course group G only: a proposed export to group H is rejected even if serialization is valid, while no live TAXII transaction occurs. Copying O1 must not raise corroboration, withdrawing S1 removes the suspicion’s support, and a protocol success changes neither attribution nor the right to share.
