Lesson 1: SIGINT metadata minimization and authority

Concept. SIGINT metadata minimization and authority frames SIGINT as authority-bound collection with minimization, handling rules, and communications-security implications.

Worked reasoning and limits. COMINT concerns intelligence from communications, including content and relevant traffic/metadata; ELINT concerns non-communications electromagnetic emissions such as radar signals, not any electronic file or cryptographic digital signature. An emission’s characteristics, an assessed emitter, a person, and intent are progressively different claims, not synonymous observations. Completed offline synthetic classification: provided card C contains a fictional message and send time (communications content plus metadata), card E describes an invented radar-like emission (ELINT-type subject matter), and card S reports a document-signature check (cryptographic authentication, not ELINT); none involves actual interception. On challenge, C’s timestamp is marked unsynchronized, so the analyst keeps the classification but withdraws the exact event-time inference and holds any consequential timing judgment. The NSA overview supports SIGINT orientation; finer operational definitions/remits need the applicable dated doctrine, and classroom classification creates no authority to acquire signals.

Why it matters. Without explicit treatment of SIGINT metadata minimization, opportunistic collection undermines requirements decomposition and source-discipline fit review; the lesson builds the habit to separate collection discipline concepts from operational recruitment, interception, or tasking procedures.

Hanyok’s historical study covering 1945–1975, not itself the complete underlying history or every contemporaneous intercept. A retrospective historian’s reconstruction, a wartime report, and a policymaker’s later testimony answer different evidentiary questions and must not be merged. The supported classroom disposition is attributed secondary reporting with the underlying-event conclusion held pending the original record, not an unqualified historical verdict.
