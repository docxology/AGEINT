Lesson 1: APT Definitions, Lifecycle, and Attribution

Concept. APT Definitions, Lifecycle, and Attribution studies APT reporting as attribution and confidence literacy: technical similarity, context, caveats, and geopolitical inference stay separate.

Worked reasoning and limits. APT is a characterization of an adversarial capability and sustained activity, not proof of a state sponsor or a universal lifecycle. Separate a publisher’s cluster definition, an observed campaign, an inferred organization and a claimed sponsor. The inherited modelling-paper citation alone does not settle those identities or validate a particular attribution; label the definition’s source and leave unsupported equivalences unresolved.

Why it matters. Lesson 7: APT Attribution: Technical Indicators vs. Geopolitical Context

Concept. APT Attribution: Technical Indicators vs. Geopolitical Context studies APT reporting as attribution and confidence literacy: technical similarity, context, caveats, and geopolitical inference stay separate.

Worked reasoning and limits. In a completed synthetic case, reports A and B repeat source S’s claim that campaign X used artifact H; record C shows H was publicly shared before X, and no record links H to an operator. Collapsing A/B to one origin and testing the shared-tool alternative yields ‘artifact overlap reported; operator and sponsor unknown’, not attribution from two reports. Removing S eliminates the overlap claim, while adding C weakens H’s discriminating value; stop before naming an actor and update if S retracts its claim. STIX can encode the competing assertions and TAXII can transport them, but those standards and ATLAS do not adjudicate factual attribution [ageint309; ageint310; ageint300].
