The Intelligent Operator as Cognitive Athlete knowledge check: misconceptions, evidence, and reviewer prompts

  1. For every topic in this module, identify a source-supported distinction, an unsupported inference to reject, and the exact evidence needed to decide. Start with Productivity Tradecraft: Sustainable Work, Review, and Recovery.
  2. Transfer the Operator-Workload Hygiene Lens artifact from Productivity Tradecraft: Sustainable Work, Review, and Recovery to the final topic, Trauma, PTSD, and Moral Injury: Awareness, Support, and Non-Diagnosis; explain what changes and what cannot transfer.
  3. Identify one failure mode from the Operator Productivity and Cognitive Performance lane and the evidence that would reveal it.
  4. Answer the coursebook review question: Which workload signal or handoff gap should be checked before attributing a quality problem to workload?
  5. Correct this misconception: that feeling certain in the moment substitutes for the checklist, pause, and second-look that decision hygiene exists to enforce.

The Intelligent Operator as Cognitive Athlete answer quality rubric: source evidence, uncertainty, and safe transfer

Judge answers with the canonical mastery evidence standard in the shared method-and-assurance reference ([@sec:method-assurance-reference]): a strong answer uses source evidence, distinguishes observation from judgment, names uncertainty, and states the safe boundary, while a revise-level answer gives a memorized definition of Productivity Tradecraft: Sustainable Work, Review, and Recovery without source evidence, uncertainty, or a safe transfer task.
