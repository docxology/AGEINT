[ageint292] Assessing Risks and Impacts of AI (ARIA): Pilot Evaluation Report The ARIA 0.1 pilot evaluation report documents NIST’s methodology for systematically assessing AI applications for risks and societal impacts, using a multi-layered evaluation approach across five participating organizations and seven submitted AI applications.
